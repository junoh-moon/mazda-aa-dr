## Content
Everything in beta.7 plus ONE behaviour change: while the vehicle is stopped (all four wheels exactly zero, incl. the 1.5 s stop-confirmation wait) the reported
accuracy is min(honest budget, 25 m) if the honest budget at the stop start was <= 150 m (decided once per stop, latched until the vehicle moves), else
the normal min(honest, 40). Journal fields acc_rule and acc_h0; analyzer tables and flip counter. See STOPPED_ACCURACY_2026-10-10.md. A deliberate under-report;
25 m and 150 m are an owner/engineering decision on single DHU observations (DHU experiments 8 and 9 Tier 1). Slow crawl (3 km/h at 40 m harmful, small accuracy
untested), stop-and-go repeats, 30/35 m, drift beyond 135 m and entrance-adjacent stops are NOT covered.

## Ranked hypotheses

1. **15 m first fixes (best supported by the data, not proven).** Every one of the 130 `location` lines in B2-r1 (and PRIME) carries accuracy
   15 m. The proven guidance-start preamble shared by experiments 6, 7, 8 and 9 (`setup-template.txt`, byte copies) sends fixes with accuracy 5 m
   before and after `tap 200 50` / `tap 345 145`. Experiment 8 ST2 accepted 15 m only after a synthetic position was already established. With
   15 m the injected position probably never replaced the phone's own location: this explains "the map stayed at the phone's location through the
   whole run", and then the two taps (which are valid only on the screen state the 5 m preamble produces) hit the wrong screen, so no guidance at t=21.
   CORRECTION (2026-10-11, after reading the full upload): an earlier version of this record called the taps themselves a blind design error
   ("established"). That was wrong: the same two taps are the proven setup of experiments 6-9. What v1 changed was the accuracy (15 m), putting
   the taps inside the measured run, and cold-starting Naver every round.
2. **Cold-start screen state (plausible, not separated).** v1 force-stops Naver and restarts DHU every round; experiments 6-9 ran the preamble on
   a warm, prepared app. The executing agent observed that a cold map has the search icon at (600,44) and that the recent destination had to be
   selected at about (200,130); a different first screen (resume prompt, 'long route recalculation' window, overlay) would make the same taps miss.
   Alternatives not separated by the data: fixes sent before the projection was ready (B3/B1 timing; B2-r1-a2 started its run file about 16 s after
   the DHU start, which argues against timing alone), or a location channel problem.
3. **Contributors (fixed or recorded, not root causes):** non-TTY controller (a1 only), session-readiness timing, the Gemini overlay.
4. **Ruled out by the data:** a package update (no change), Naver cold start as such (exp7 H6 worked), the 30 s wait (exp7 used 30-47 s),
   the controller as the only cause (native replay failed the same way). P0 being far from the prepared destination is not a cause of
   "no guidance" (the destination list does not depend on the position) but would show as a recalculation popup, which v1 never handled.

One diagnostic separates hypothesis 2 from its alternatives: in one fresh session, after the projection is ready and before any UI input,
send 15 s of P0 fixes at 15 m, screenshot, then 15 s at 5 m, screenshot (v2 Tier 0 `T0-LPROBE`: LP-15 / LP-5 / LP-NONE).

## What changed in the plan (v2, superseded by v3 below)

- Run files carry no taps. Each fresh session first passes gate A (Naver map visible, Naver process alive, no call, no overlay), then
  streams `PREP-<condition>.txt` (the condition's own standstill fix with accuracy 5 m) while the agent starts guidance with coordinates
  resolved from the screen (uiautomator if it sees the projection, else OCR boxes, else icon templates, else the observed fallback
  coordinates), with gate L (map moved to P0) and gate G (OCR: remaining 1.0-1.9 km and an ETA token; direct reading: blue arrow, route
  line). The run file follows without a gap. The R label is read from the gate-G photo and t=0-31; D0 is the gate-G remaining distance.
- Tier 0 smoke test (about 6-9 min) before any experimental run: T0-LPROBE, then PREP-B2 + guidance + 30 s of the B2 run start. On failure
  one exp9-start reference session, then ABORT with a diagnostic bundle (no experimental run is started).
- Bounded retries per step and per session; 2 consecutive rounds FAILED with the same reason stops the loop; cold-preparation failures
  first try a documented warm fallback (DHU-only restart, guidance kept), whose limits are written into the plan (no R label, held heading
  near T may mimic S-TRUE, U-2 not concluded from W runs).
- Budget 3 h (was 3 h 30 min). Conditions, inputs after t=0, photo times, S and M labels and the decision table are unchanged.
- Owner: USB debugging was switched off and the ADB key deleted by the v1 agent's cleanup, so the owner must re-enable USB debugging and
  accept the ADB prompt before the night run; the v2 prompt forbids changing phone settings at cleanup.

## Addendum: the photos (owner upload, read by the analyst, 2026-10-11)

Observation (screens): in every inspected frame (B2-r1 a1, a2 and a3 at t=0, 6 and 21; B1-r1 a1 at t=55; the environment-prime and
prime-verified screens) the Naver map stayed at the phone's real location (the owner's physical place, not the Busan test site), with the
blue arrow there. The injected test positions never moved the map. The PRIME at 15 m also left the map at the real location. At t=21 Naver
showed the free map with the home, search, pin and settings buttons: no guidance and no route.

Interpretation: the 15 m injection of v1 never displaced the phone's own fix. This supports hypothesis 1 (15 m first fixes) as the lead
explanation and makes the taps moot: guidance cannot start correctly from a map that is not at the test site, whatever the taps hit. It
is still not proven that 5 m would have worked in the same cold state; that is what v3 Tier 0 (proven preamble at 5 m) and, if it fails,
T0-LPROBE show.

Side observation from the exp7-exp9 bundles (relevant to the v3 fallback only): their executing agents often adapted the preparation
after looking at the screen (the observed 모라역 row at x 230 and y 130 / 225 / 318, a second route-start tap, dismissing a recalculation
popup at (170, 177)) while sending the preamble's 5 m fix; exp6 used the template taps as written except one run with a changed recent
list. The template remains the primary path in v3; these observed adaptations are its fallback.

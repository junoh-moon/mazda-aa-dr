## Ranked hypotheses

1. **Guidance-start procedure (established, sufficient alone).** Two blind taps at an obsolete coordinate, no destination-row tap, no
   popup dismissal, no gate. Guidance could not start; this explains "no guidance at t=21" in all 9 attempts.
2. **15 m first fixes at a cold session do not replace the phone's own location (supported, not proven).** Explains "map stayed at the
   phone's location" through the whole run including the 15 m motion phase. Alternatives not separated by the data: fixes sent before the
   projection was ready (B3/B1 timing), or a location channel problem; B2-r1-a2 started its run file about 16 s after the DHU start, which argues against
   timing alone.
3. **Contributors (fixed or recorded, not root causes):** non-TTY controller (a1 only), session-readiness timing, the Gemini overlay.
4. **Ruled out by the data:** a package update (no change), Naver cold start as such (exp7 H6 worked), the 30 s wait (exp7 used 30-47 s),
   the controller as the only cause (native replay failed the same way). P0 being far from the prepared destination is not a cause of
   "no guidance" (the destination list does not depend on the position) but would show as a recalculation popup, which v1 never handled.

One diagnostic separates hypothesis 2 from its alternatives: in one fresh session, after the projection is ready and before any UI input,
send 15 s of P0 fixes at 15 m, screenshot, then 15 s at 5 m, screenshot (v2 Tier 0 `T0-LPROBE`: LP-15 / LP-5 / LP-NONE).

## What changed in the plan (v2)

- Run files carry no taps. Each fresh session first passes gate A (Naver map visible, Naver process alive, no call, no overlay), then
  streams `PREP-<condition>.txt` (the condition's own standstill fix with accuracy 5 m) while the agent starts guidance with coordinates
  resolved from the screen (uiautomator if it sees the projection, else OCR boxes, else icon templates, else the observed fallback
  coordinates), with gate L (map moved to P0) and gate G (OCR: remaining 1.0-1.9 km and an ETA token; direct reading: blue arrow, route
  line). The run file follows without a gap. The R label is read from the gate-G photo and t=0-31; D0 is the gate-G remaining distance.
- Tier 0 smoke test (about 6-9 min) before any experimental run: T0-LPROBE, then PREP-B2 + guidance + 30 s of the B2 run start. On failure
  one exp9-start reference session, then ABORT with a diagnostic bundle (no experimental run is started).
- Bounded retries per step and per session; 2 consecutive rounds FAILED with the same reason stops the loop; cold-preparation failures
  first try a documented warm fallback (DHU-only restart, guidance kept), whose limits are written into the plan (no R label, held heading
  near T may mimic S-TRUE, U-2 not concluded from W runs).
- Budget 3 h (was 3 h 30 min). Conditions, inputs after t=0, photo times, S and M labels and the decision table are unchanged.
- Owner: USB debugging was switched off and the ADB key deleted by the v1 agent's cleanup, so the owner must re-enable USB debugging and
  accept the ADB prompt before the night run; the v2 prompt forbids changing phone settings at cleanup.

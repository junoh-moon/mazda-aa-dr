digest fields), log-only capture of the VIM side channels 0x116 +4..+9, 0x169 and 0x15B (tap change inside the stock jciVBS process; separate non-blocking
socket; marker-file off switch via trial menu 7, read once at start), analyzer sections. See YAW_DATA_COLLECTION_2026-10-09.md and
VIM_CHANNEL_CAPTURE_2026-10-10.md.

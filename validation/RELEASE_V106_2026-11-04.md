## Procedure

This is the same manual procedure as v1.0.4 and v1.0.5. The release commit is not an ancestor of origin/master, so G0 `setup.sh` was not used.

1. **Build (G1).** In a fresh clone, `1120675` was checked out detached; `git status --porcelain` was empty. `tools/build_arm.py` with the pinned toolchain reported `Verified ARM build`.
2. **Comparison with the published v1.0.5 ZIP.** The v1.0.5 ZIP was downloaded again and passed its sidecar check. `cmp` against it shows:
   - `libmx5dr.so`, `libmx5dr-vimtap.so`, `libmx5dr-ldstap.so`, `mx5dr-guard` and `mx5dr-sha256` are identical;
   - only `mx5dr-collector` differs, which is the intended change.

   The file lists of the two ZIPs are identical.
3. **ZIP.** `make_usb_zip.py --default-mode BETA`. The build-info has `source_commit=1120675…`, `source_modified=false`, `version=1.0.6`, `default_mode=BETA`. The internal SHA256SUMS check passes.
4. **G5/G6.** `g5g6_zipcheck.py --commit 1120675… --mode BETA --version 1.0.6 --readelf <pinned readelf>` passes **68/68**. The collector gained a NEEDED entry for `libdl.so.2`; it does not link libstdc++ dynamically.
5. **Packaging suite against the real ZIP.** With `MX5DR_RELEASE_BUNDLE` pointing at the unpacked ZIP, `tests/packaging` ran **533 tests, OK, 103 skipped**.
   - Without a host build, the 65 compressed-path tests and the host collector tests were skipped.
   - The other skips are the same categories as v1.0.5: no stock identity fixture, and no pinned ARM compiler path for the bds parsing.
6. **Branch `release/v1.0.6` with a host build.**
   - `make test-collector`: 51 OK, including the journal unit tests and the 5 storage scenarios.
   - `tests/packaging`: **533 OK, 48 skipped**, including the 65 `CompressedExportTests`.
   - `make test-runtime`: OK.
   - `make test-recovery`: 10 OK.
   - `tests/tools/test_analyze_logs.py`: 38 OK.
7. **ARM emulation (qemu-arm 8.2.2).** Runtime: the toolchain sysroot glibc 2.11 plus Debian armel zlib 1.2.7.
   - The release ZIP's `mx5dr-collector --gzip`/`--gunzip` round-trips a vehicle collector slice byte-identically, and Python gzip decodes the output too.
   - Before the base correction, on the same source change:
     - the ARM `test_collector_journal` passed;
     - `test_trial_menu` passed with BusyBox 1.20.2 applets: 126 OK;
     - with the ARM collector as the helper, 65 OK.
8. **Publication.** The branch and the annotated tag were pushed, then `gh release create v1.0.6 --verify-tag --latest`.
9. **G18 re-download.**
   - The sidecar `sha256sum -c` passes.
   - `cmp` of the downloaded ZIP and APK against the built files: identical.
   - `unzip -t` reports no errors, and the internal SHA256SUMS check passes.
